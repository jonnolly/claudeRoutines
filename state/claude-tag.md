# `@claude` tag — what it means and what carries it

Applied 2026-09-21. Tag created in TickTick as name `@claude`, label `@Claude`.

## The rule

A task carries `@claude` when **a written answer is the whole deliverable** —
research, comparison, calculation, a reading list, a plan on paper. The drain
routine (Tip 001) answers these and comments back.

Deliberately **not** tagged:

- Anything needing his hands or his presence — buy, book, call, pay, collect,
  attend, practise, rehearse, transcribe.
- Anything needing another person — "ask Frank", "message Kymé", "ask Sam".
- Filing into Obsidian. He has ruled out agent writes to the vault, so
  `#addtoobsidian` capture tasks stay his. (Where such a task also contained a
  real research question, it is tagged — the answer comes back as a comment and
  he files it himself.)
- Listening/watching tasks. Claude cannot hear a Spotify timestamp.
- **Coding work** — Gratitude Journal (23 open) and Trace Trimmer (7 open).
  These are real Claude work but a different kind: they need a repo, a branch
  and a diff, not a comment. Mixing them into the drain routine would break it.
  Awaiting his decision on a separate tag.
- Counselling and relationship material. Sensitive, and not tagged without
  him saying so.
