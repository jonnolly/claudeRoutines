## Parked — too big for a 15-minute daily slot

These need a proper sit-down. Propose as a separate weekly/monthly routine that **he**
schedules, per his standing instruction.

- P1 — Map every recurring task in your week and mark which are delegable to an agent.
- P2 — Decide a personal policy on what you will *never* delegate (judgement, client comms, music).
- P3 — Design the freelance funnel end-to-end (profile, proposal templates, portfolio, pricing).
- P4 — Audit: what did Claude actually save you this month, in hours and euros?
